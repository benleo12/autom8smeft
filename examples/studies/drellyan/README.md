# Drell-Yan study

High-mass neutral-current Drell-Yan at 13.6 TeV, `p p > e+ e-` and `p p > e+ e- a`, both with the
lepton pair above 200 GeV. This is the process that reaches the four-fermion classes. 311 of the
674 operators sit in classes 18 to 21, none of them enters diboson or triboson production, and the
vector-boson-fusion study leaves classes 18, 20 and 21 out on cost, so without this study nearly
half the basis would appear in no cross section. Class 19, psi^4 X, needs the extra photon and gives no diagram in
`p p > e+ e-` alone, which is why the second process is there.
