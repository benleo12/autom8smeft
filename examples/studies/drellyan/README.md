# Drell-Yan study

High-mass neutral-current Drell-Yan at 13.6 TeV, `p p > e+ e-` and `p p > e+ e- a`, both with the
lepton pair above 200 GeV. This is the process that reaches the four-fermion classes: 311 of the
674 operators sit in classes 18 to 21, and none of them enters diboson, triboson or Higgs-pair
production in vector boson fusion, so without this study nearly half the basis would never have
appeared in a cross section. Class 19, psi^4 X, needs the extra photon and gives no diagram in
`p p > e+ e-` alone, which is why the second process is there.
