# High-mass neutral-current Drell-Yan, the process that opens the four-fermion classes.
# mmll is the invariant mass of the same-flavour opposite-sign lepton pair, which is what e+ e-
# is, so the cut applies (for a different-flavour pair it silently does nothing: use mmnl).
p p > e+ e- | mmll=200
# ptgmin is not a run-card entry: the photon pT cut is pta and the photon-lepton
# separation is dral.  The first spelling made set_cards.py reject every class row.
p p > e+ e- a | mmll=200 pta=20 dral=0.4
