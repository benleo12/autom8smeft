# draws as horizontal lines (--lines): a label, the value and its source, separated by tabs.

# FCC-ee does not quote a projection for the partial width itself.  The value here is ours, from the
# projected errors of Table 2 of the Feasibility Study, statistical and systematic added in quadrature.
# With lepton universality Gamma_ll = M_Z Gamma_Z sqrt(sigma_had^0 / (12 pi R_l)), so its relative error is
# that of Gamma_Z, half that of sigma_had^0 and half that of R_l added in quadrature: 12.6 keV of
# 2495500 keV, 0.80 pb of 41480.2 pb and 0.071e-3 of 20.767 give 1.1e-5, without the correlations.  The
# same sum with the present errors of that table gives 1.2e-3, against the 1.0e-3 of the PDG fit of
# Gamma(l+ l-) (83.984 +- 0.086 MeV).
