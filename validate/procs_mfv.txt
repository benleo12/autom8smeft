# Ward-identity processes for the flavour structures of block DIM8F, for validate/mg5_gluon_check.sh with
# WARD_INPUTS=generic, beside validate/procs_ew.txt and the gluon list.  The structures carry Y Y^dag on a
# fermion current (light quarks and leptons, and the top and bottom quarks), Y_u^dag Y_d on a right-handed
# charged current, the second flavour contraction of four-fermion operators with two identical currents, and
# y^4 on both bilinears, so the processes have external t and b, light fermions of every generation, charged
# currents across generations through the Cabibbo angle, and four external fermions of two flavours of one
# kind, each with an external photon or gluon whose polarisation a boost shifts by its momentum.  On the
# default card, light fermions massless and cabi = 0, every structure that needs a light Yukawa coupling or
# the Cabibbo angle vanishes in these processes, so the list is for generic inputs.
# a fermion current with a Yukawa insertion, by flavour
d d~ > a z
s s~ > h a
c c~ > h g
b b~ > a h
t t~ > a h
g g > t t~
mu+ mu- > a z
ta+ ta- > a h
# right-handed and cross-generation charged currents
u s~ > w+ a
c s~ > w+ g
t b~ > w+ a
ta+ vt > w+ a
# four external fermions: crossed contractions of two currents of one kind, insertions on both, and y^4
u u~ > t t~ g
d d~ > b b~ a
u u~ > c c~ g
u u~ > e+ e- a
u d~ > e+ ve a
e+ e- > ta+ ta- a
